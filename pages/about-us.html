Our Story & Founder's Vision

Serving Every Traveler with Devotion, Integrity & Precision

Named with deep reverence to Lord Shiva, Shivaputhra Travels commenced its journey in 2022. Founded with a mission to simplify long-distance travel, we set out to ensure that every temple pilgrimage, family holiday, and corporate trip is executed with total peace of mind, immaculate vehicle hygiene, and absolute punctuality.

Over the years, we have specialized in high-demand sacred Yatra circuits—including same-day Tirupati Balaji VIP Darshans, Aarupadai Veedu Murugan Yatras, Navagraha Temples of Kumbakonam, and Rameswaram–Kanyakumari–Madurai circuits. We take complete responsibility for route optimization, driver batta clarity, and highway family rest-stops so you can focus entirely on your prayers.

Beyond pilgrimages, Shivaputhra Travels operates a luxury corporate fleet (Innova Hycross, Camry, Force Urbania) serving top IT enterprises, conventions, and family hill station holidays across Ooty, Kodaikanal, Munnar, and Coonoor.
